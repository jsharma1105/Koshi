## Key Insights
1. BM25 dominates at small corpus size (12-24 chunks) — keyword overlap is high
2. Vector search benefits from moderate chunk sizes (256-384) — too small loses context, too large dilutes signal
3. Overlap has minimal impact at small scale — becomes critical with larger documents
4. RRF-k is robust across values — fusion quality matters more than the smoothing constant
5. LLM reranking with phi4-mini DEGRADES results (MRR 0.967→0.624) — use stronger models or skip
